How it works

Step 1 — Log in (once): Click Setup Login. A browser opens; log in to your subscription and close it. Cookies are saved for reuse.
Fallback: If the browser login is blocked, log in via your normal browser, then copy the 'cookie' header from DevTools (F12 → Network → 'reader') and paste it above.
Step 2 — Harvest: Pick an Edition and Date, then click Start Ingestion. DHĪ pulls every article's text straight from The Hindu's edition API (no screenshots or OCR) and writes a single structured Markdown note to your vault, grouped by section.
